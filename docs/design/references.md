# Reference board — the Chamber, lit

Every mockup, primitive and motion in BoltVault is reviewed against this board (master plan §7.12). It exists so "jaw-dropping" is a comparison, not an adjective.

## Subject

Electroneum is electricity as money: a chain with a real 5-second pulse, a currency named after a unit of energy, a DEX whose mark is a bolt. BoltVault is an instrument you look *through* — smoked glass over a live electric field.

## What it must evoke

| Reference | What we take from it | What we leave |
|---|---|---|
| **Analogue instrument panels** (bench multimeters, aircraft standby gauges, Tektronix scopes) | Engraved labels, one readout that dominates, plates with physical roles (seat, readout, bus, rack, fuse), honest needles that move only when the value does | Beige plastic, skeuomorphic screws |
| **Oscilloscope phosphor** (P31 green → we use the arc spectrum) | A live trace that decays, a glow that comes from *light behind glass* rather than a CSS drop-shadow, digits that read at a glance | Green; scanlines as decoration |
| **Teenage Engineering hardware** (OP-1, TX-6, the Field series) | Restraint; a small number of controls, each with a real job; type as part of the object; joy without cartoonishness | Orange as a brand colour |
| **Aurora and long-exposure lightning photography** | The Field: filaments with a white-blue core, violet fringe, warm edge; depth from layered light, never from grey shadows | Sky blue; photo textures |
| **Smoked-glass dashboards** (Bang & Olufsen, Devialet) | Black glass that is never flat: vignette, grain, a light source that moves; plates rise or recess, they do not "float with a shadow" | Chrome bezels |
| **Physical breakers and fuse boxes** | The custody vocabulary is *visual only*: a labelled key, a fuse that reads as something you pull; nothing swipes to throw | The words "breaker", "fuse box", "discharge" in copy (§7.10) |

## What it must not resemble

| Wallet | Why it is the trap |
|---|---|
| **Phantom** | Navy + violet + a friendly mascot — the exact "second accent" and "pill" language that reads as a template once copied |
| **Zerion** | Gradient cards on near-black; identical rounded rectangles with one radius; motion on every card |
| **Backpack** | Terminal-chic monospace labels and neon-on-black; the "dev tool" register the plan bans |

If a screen could be mistaken for any of the three with the logo removed, it fails.

## The one memorable element

**The Field** (§7.6): an address-seeded flow field of faint arcs behind the glass, brightening 120 ms on each Electroneum block when home is ETN, bending toward touch, sending a discharge across on confirm. Everything else on the screen is quiet so the Field can be loud. The seat avatar is a 40 px crop of the same field, so an account is recognisable by its light.

## Signature moments (acceptance in §7.12)

1. **Ignition** — unlock: the Field fades in around the seat avatar, the filament draws left→right, the readout digits settle from blurred to sharp; 400 ms; a device powering on, not a page loading.
2. **Discharge** — Swap: an arc leaves the Swap key, crosses the Field, lands as the new Activity row; ≤ 600 ms; the eye is led to where the result lives.
3. **The engraved QR** — Receive on mobile: etched into glass with gyroscope parallax on the light; static on web; scans first time at 30 cm.
4. **The Rack** — Collectibles on glass shelves with depth; a piece opens by shared element and is lit by one slow sweep.
5. **The Coil** — a farm ring that hums brighter as DYNO accrues; dates engraved; Collect discharges into the readout.
6. **The Legends vault** — claimable ETN rising as liquid light; Claim drains it into the readout.

## Calibration (what we refuse)

Near-black with one neon accent and identical rounded cards; tracked all-caps eyebrows; `A · B · C` meta strings; `01 / 02 / 03` where nothing is a sequence; `→` appended to buttons; monospace for small labels; sad-mascot empty states; a second accent colour in chrome. Paint palette is §7.3; `plasma` is light only.
